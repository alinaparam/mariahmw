Rule · remember

to be или do / does?

to be · am / is / areглагол · do / does
когдакто ты, какой, где: a guest, nervous, rich, in the bar, from Londonчто ты делаешь: smoke, work, live, have, know
+She is nervous.She smokes.
−She isn’t nervous.She doesn’t smoke.
?Is she nervous?Does she smoke?
✓ / ✗Yes, she is. / No, she isn’t.Yes, she does. / No, she doesn’t.

❗ Две ловушки

1. nervous, rich, angry, tired — это не глаголы, а «какой». Значит: Do you nervous? → Are you nervous?

2. После does / doesn’t у глагола нет -s: Does she smokes? → Does she smoke? · He doesn’t has → He doesn’t have
